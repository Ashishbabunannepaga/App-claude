CapitUp India · event booth · concept pack v1

A 6-foot photo booth that makes reading your policy worth a selfie.

Three concepts for a freestanding 1.8 m booth, drawn to scale. Concept A is recommended and has print-ready files. Every item marked Needs approval must be signed off by CapitUp before printing.

Logo: the official CapitUp logo file is not in the project, so every logo position is an exact-size, labelled box and nothing is redrawn. Put the approved vector master at marketing/photo-booth/print/logo/capitup-logo.svg and re-run the build. The logo then goes in at its own proportions, so its colours and spacing stay exactly as supplied. The logo always sits on a light plate because the teal in the logo would get lost on the dark booth. Needs approval

Look and feel

The base is matte deep ink with gold as the only accent. Warm 2700 K LED light runs along the edges. Light plates carry the logo and the QR code. Type is Inter, the font the CapitUp app already uses.

How a visitor uses it

  1. Spot itThe lit top sign is visible over the crowd. One host stands at the booth and asks, “Want a photo?”
  2. Grab a propThe visitor picks up the “My Policy Report” card. It is a sample that shows no real policy and no personal data.
  3. Stand in the frameUp to three people stand on the “Stand here” floor decal inside the gold brackets.
  4. Take the photo on their own phoneThe visitor clips their phone onto the stand over the “Phone goes here” floor spot, 1.8 m from the frame, then taps the Bluetooth shutter or uses the timer. The photo never leaves their phone, and CapitUp stores nothing.
  5. Share itThey post the photo with #PolicyChecked and tag CapitUp’s account. Needs approval (hashtag and handle)
  6. Scan the QR codeWith the phone still in hand, they scan the QR code on the light plate to the right of the frame. capitupindia.com opens in the browser.
  7. Stick your winThey take two stickers: one to keep and one for the sticker wall, which asks, “What did your policy surprise you with?”
No client data at the booth. Hosts don't collect policy numbers, phone numbers or documents, and they don't photograph visitors on company devices. Anyone who wants a review signs up on the website, where consent is captured. On the morning of each event, scan the QR code with one iPhone and one Android phone from 3 m away.

What CapitUp needs to approve

  • The official logo vector, clear-space and minimum-size rules, and whether there is an approved on-dark version
  • The tagline “Check your policy. Share your win.”
  • The supporting lines: “We read the fine print, so you don't have to.” · “Policy review, in plain words” · “Scan to check your policy” · “We don't sell insurance.” Check them against what the service actually does and against IRDAI advertising norms.
  • The hashtag #PolicyChecked and the social handle to tag
  • The six sticker lines and the sample report card content
  • The QR destination: currently plain https://capitupindia.com. Add UTM tags if event tracking is wanted.
  • Colour proofs on the real materials, the budget and vendor choice, and the venue rules (power, floor decals, fire-retardant fabric certificate)
